James Webb Space Telescope

NASA / ESA / CSA

Mankind's most powerful space telescope, its 6.5-metre gold-beryllium mirror array peers 13.5 billion years into the past to observe the first galaxies formed after the Big Bang, stationed 1.5 million km from Earth.

ORBIT Halo L2

Optical Observatory

Hubble Space Telescope

NASA / ESA

Orbiting since 1990 at 540 km altitude, Hubble has executed over 1.5 million observations and produced more than 19,000 peer-reviewed papers. Its imagery of deep fields revealed a universe containing over two trillion galaxies.

ORBIT LEO 540 km
LAUNCHED 1990
NORAD 20580

Sentinel-2

ESA / Copernicus Programme

Twin Earth-imaging satellites providing 10-metre multispectral optical imagery across 13 spectral bands every 5 days. Monitors agriculture, deforestation, disaster response, coastal changes, and urban growth globally.

ORBIT Polar SSO 786 km

RESOLUTION 10 m

Weather

GOES-18 (GOES-West)

NOAA / NASA

Advanced geostationary weather satellite observing the Western Hemisphere from 35,786 km altitude. Captures full-disc Earth imagery every 10 minutes in 16 spectral bands, enabling real-time hurricane, storm, and wildfire tracking.

ORBIT GEO 35,786 km
LAUNCHED 2022
POSITION 137.2°W

The latest in the 50-year Landsat Earth observation program. OLI-2 instrument captures 11 spectral bands at 30-metre resolution, contributing to the world's longest continuous land surface record for climate and resource science.

X-ray Observatory

Chandra X-ray Observatory

NASA / CXC / SAO

NASA's flagship X-ray space telescope occupying a highly elliptical orbit reaching 1/3 of the way to the Moon. Its nested iridium-coated mirror arrays resolve X-ray emission from black hole accretion disks, supernova remnants, and galaxy clusters.

ORBIT HEO 134,500 km
LAUNCHED 1999
NORAD 25867
▸ Chandra Center
Exoplanet Hunter

TESS — Transiting Exoplanet Survey

NASA / MIT

Four wide-field cameras scan 85% of the sky for transiting exoplanets orbiting nearby bright stars. TESS has discovered 7,000+ exoplanet candidates including habitable-zone terrestrial worlds, orbiting in a unique 2:1 resonance orbit with the Moon.

ORBIT Lunar Resonance HEO
LAUNCHED 2018
FOV 2,300 sq° per sector
▸ TESS Mission
Deep Space Probe

Cassini-Huygens

NASA / ESA / ASI

Flagship mission orbiting Saturn for 13 years, discovering ocean plumes on Enceladus, methane lakes on Titan, and mapping Saturn's ring system in unprecedented detail. Concluded with a planned Grand Finale atmospheric dive in 2017.

TARGET Saturn System
ACTIVE 1997–2017
STATUS Mission Complete

Gravity Science

GRACE-FO

NASA / GFZ Potsdam / DLR

Twin satellites flying 220 km apart, measuring micro-variations in Earth's gravitational field via laser ranging interferometry. Maps glacial melting, groundwater depletion, and sea level change with millimetre-precision monthly gravity maps.

ORBIT Polar LEO 490 km
LAUNCHED 2018
SEPARATION 220 km
