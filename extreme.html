Extreme Space

The most violent, dense, and energetic phenomena in the universe

EXTREME 01 • GRAVITATIONAL SINGULARITY

BLACK HOLE

Regions of spacetime where gravitational collapse reaches infinite density. Within the boundary known as the event horizon, escape velocity exceeds the speed of light, trapping matter and radiation forever while warping space and time around its searing accretion disk.

Event HorizonBoundary of No Return
Central SingularityInfinite Density & Zero Volume
Milky Way Black HoleSagittarius A* (4.3M Solar Masses)
Escape Velocity> 299,792 km/s (Exceeds Light)
EXTREME 02 • STELLAR DETONATION

SUPERNOVA

The cataclysmic explosive death of a dying massive star. In a matter of seconds, a supernova unleashes as much raw energy as our Sun will emit across its entire 10-billion-year lifespan, outshining an entire host galaxy and forging heavy elements essential for life.

Peak LuminosityOutshines 100 Billion Stars
Debris VelocityUp to 30,000 km/s (10% Speed of Light)
Core RemnantNeutron Star or Black Hole
Elements ForgedIron, Gold, Platinum, Uranium
EXTREME 03 • ULTRA-DENSE CORE

NEUTRON STAR

The hyper-condensed stellar corpse left behind by a supernova. Packing twice the mass of our Sun into a sphere only 20 kilometers wide, its atoms are crushed into pure neutron matter. Rapidly rotating pulsars beam sweeping relativistic radiation beacons across interstellar space.

Core Diameter~20 km (City-Sized)
Matter Density100 Trillion g/cm³ (1 tsp = 1 Billion Tons)
Spin FrequencyUp to 700+ Revolutions / sec
Magnetic FieldUp to 1 Trillion Gauss
EXTREME 04 • COSMIC BEACON

QUASAR

Quasi-stellar beacons powered by ravenous supermassive black holes gorging on interstellar gas and dust at galactic cores. Intense gravitational friction heats infalling accretion disks to hundreds of millions of degrees, erupting twin relativistic particle jets that pierce deep cosmic space.

Total LuminosityUp to 1,000x Entire Milky Way Galaxy
Plasma Jet LengthHundreds of Thousands of Light-Years
Disk Temperature> 100 Million °C
Cosmic DistanceBillions of Light-Years from Earth
EXTREME 05 • HIGH-ENERGY FLASH

GAMMA-RAY BURST

The most energetic and blinding explosive events observed in the known universe. Triggered during catastrophic collapses of supermassive stars into new black holes (hypernovae) or violent neutron star collisions (kilonovae), firing narrow beams of pure high-energy gamma rays across the cosmos.

Peak RadiationHigh-Energy Gamma Rays (>100 keV)
Burst DurationMilliseconds to Several Minutes
Energy Output> 10⁴⁴ Joules (Sun's Lifetime in Seconds)
Primary OriginHypernova Core Collapse & Kilonova Mergers
