Famous Space Missions

Apollo 11 (1969)

First human landing on the Moon by NASA, carrying astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin.

Voyager 1 & 2 (1977)

Explored outer planets and became the first human-made objects to enter interstellar space.

Hubble Telescope (1990)

Revolutionary space observatory capturing high-resolution images of deep stars and galaxies.

Curiosity Rover (2012)

Car-sized Mars rover studying Martian climate, geology, and past conditions for life.

Chandrayaan-3 (2023)

Historic ISRO mission that achieved the world's first soft landing near the Moon's South Pole.

James Webb (2021)

Most powerful infrared space telescope, peering back over 13.5 billion years into early universe.
