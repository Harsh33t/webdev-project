Space Missions

A visual timeline charting humanity's greatest expeditions across our solar system and into interstellar space.

1969
NASA • CREWED

Apollo 11

First Human Lunar Landing

Commander Neil Armstrong and Lunar Module Pilot Buzz Aldrin landed the Eagle in the Sea of Tranquility, fulfilling humanity's dream of walking on another world.

Destination: The MoonStatus: Completed
1977
NASA / JPL • ROBOTIC

Voyager 1 & 2

Outer Planets Grand Tour

Twin probes dispatched to survey Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. Both spacecraft have now crossed the heliopause into pristine interstellar space over 24 billion km away.

Destination: Interstellar VoidStatus: Transmitting
1990
NASA / ESA • ORBITAL

Hubble Space Telescope

Revolutionizing Cosmic Vision

Orbiting above Earth's obscuring atmosphere, Hubble has executed over 1.5 million observations, determining the expansion rate of the universe and capturing iconic deep-field galaxies.

Orbit: Low Earth (540 km)Status: Operational
2011
NASA • MARS EXPLORATION

Curiosity Rover

Searching for Ancient Habitability

Nuclear-powered robotic rover exploring Mount Sharp inside Gale Crater, confirming that ancient Mars had liquid freshwater lakes capable of supporting microbial life.

Destination: Mars SurfaceStatus: Active Exploration
2021
NASA / ESA / CSA • DEEP SPACE

James Webb (JWST)

Peering into the Cosmic Dawn

Equipped with a 6.5-meter gold beryllium primary mirror, Webb observes the early universe in deep infrared, detecting the very first galaxies formed 13.5 billion years ago.

Position: Sun-Earth L2 PointStatus: Flagship Science
2023
ISRO • LUNAR EXPEDITION

Chandrayaan-3

Historic South Pole Touchdown

ISRO's Vikram lander and Pragyan rover accomplished humanity's first successful soft landing near the uncharted lunar South Pole, probing lunar soil temperatures and seismic activity.

Destination: Lunar South PoleStatus: Mission Accomplished
