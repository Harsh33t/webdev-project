The Planets

Mercury

Closest planet to the Sun. Small, rocky world with cratered plains.

Venus

Hottest planet in the solar system covered with thick sulfuric clouds.

Earth

Our home world with liquid water oceans and a protective atmosphere.

Mars

The Red Planet, home to Olympus Mons volcano and iron-rich dusty soil.

Jupiter

The largest gas giant planet, famous for its Great Red Spot storm.

Saturn

Second largest planet, encircled by thousands of bright icy rings.

Uranus

An icy blue giant that rotates completely on its side.

Neptune

The outermost giant planet with super-fast winds and dark deep storms.
