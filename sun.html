A STAR LIKE NO OTHER

The Sun is a G-type main-sequence star, commonly classified as a G2V star. It formed approximately 4.6 billion years ago from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust. Like other stars, it produces energy through nuclear fusion in its core.

CONVECTIVE ZONE

PHOTOSPHERE

CHROMOSPHERE

CORONA

The Sun's extremely hot outer atmosphere.

The Sun's outer corona can reach temperatures far hotter than its visible surface.
